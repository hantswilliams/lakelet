What stays on your laptop, what moves, and who can see it.

Lakelet grows from one laptop to a company without changing where the data lives. Three setups, one rule: data files are always in your storage, Lakelet's cloud only ever holds metadata, and anything that leaves your machine is something you turned on.

One person. Everything on the laptop until you say otherwise.

Free · Local

A solo engineer, a learner, or a founder with a CSV. There is no account, no server and no network call until you connect a bucket or a model key. Burst is available on the free tier; it needs a sign-in and a bucket, nothing else.

With no bucket and no key configured, Lakelet makes zero network requests. That is checkable, and the beta ships a `lakelet audit network` command that proves it.

Two to ten people. One catalog, one bucket, everyone's laptop.

Team · $24 per seat

The team catalog is the same Iceberg REST catalog, hosted on Postgres. It holds table metadata and hands out short-lived, prefix-scoped credentials to your bucket. Laptops still run most queries locally; scheduled dbt runs and compaction happen on workers in your account.

Local queries still never leave the laptop. The catalog decides who may read a table; the bucket does the reading.

A company. Lakelet's cloud moves into your account.

Self-hosted · from $25K / yr

The same catalog and control plane, deployed into your VPC with Terraform. SSO, table-level roles, an audit log to your SIEM, a warm worker pool, several buckets in several regions. Lakelet ships software and updates and sees nothing else. Laptops still run most queries locally, because that is still the fastest place.
